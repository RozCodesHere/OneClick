How do I book a service?

Choose a service, review available provider information, select a suitable booking time, and submit your booking request.

Can I cancel a booking?

Booking cancellation can be managed according to the booking status and the options available in the OneClick platform.

How do I become a provider?

Create a provider account, complete your provider profile, and wait for administrator verification before accepting bookings.
