Quick Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Can't find what you're looking for? Check our full FAQ page for more details.

How do I book a service?

Choose a service, pick a verified provider, select a time slot, and confirm — all in under a minute.

How do payments work?

Pay securely in-app. Funds stay in escrow and are released to the provider once the job is done.

Can I cancel a booking?

Yes, bookings can be cancelled or rescheduled free of charge up to 2 hours before the scheduled time.

How do I become a provider?

Apply online, complete verification, and start accepting bookings once your profile is approved.

How do refunds work?

Eligible refunds are processed back to your original payment method within 3–5 business days.
