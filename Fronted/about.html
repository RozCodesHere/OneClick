Our Story

From an Idea to Nepal's Home Service Platform

OneClick was built to solve a problem every household in Nepal knows too well — finding a reliable, verified professional at the right time, for the right price.

Why We Started

Finding a trustworthy plumber, electrician, or cleaner in Nepal often meant asking around, hoping for the best, and negotiating blindly. We saw a gap between skilled local professionals and the customers who needed them — and no simple way to bridge it.

Problems We Solve

Finding and managing local services can be inconvenient when information is scattered across different sources. OneClick brings service categories, provider profiles, service details, bookings, and customer reviews together in one platform.

Our Mission

To provide a simple platform where customers can discover local services, view provider information, make bookings, and manage their service requests from one place.

Our Vision

To develop OneClick into a practical local service marketplace with a clear, user-friendly experience for both customers and service providers.

Our Journey So Far

2026
Project Concept

The OneClick concept was developed as a semester project to explore how local service discovery and booking could be managed through a web-based marketplace.

2026
Feature Planning

The project scope was defined around customers, service providers, services, bookings, reviews, and administrative management.

2026
Backend Development

Django REST Framework and PostgreSQL were used to develop the project's core APIs, authentication, service, provider, booking, and review functionality.

2026
Frontend Integration

The frontend was connected to the backend APIs using HTML, CSS, Bootstrap, and JavaScript so that pages can display real application data.

Future Improvements

Future work can focus on expanding service categories, improving booking workflows, strengthening dashboards, and extending the platform's service coverage.
