Our Story

From an Idea to Nepal's Home Service Platform

OneClick was built to solve a problem every household in Nepal knows too well — finding a reliable, verified professional at the right time, for the right price.

Why We Started

Finding a trustworthy plumber, electrician, or cleaner in Nepal often meant asking around, hoping for the best, and negotiating blindly. We saw a gap between skilled local professionals and the customers who needed them — and no simple way to bridge it.

Problems We Solve

Unverified workers, unclear pricing, missed appointments, and no accountability. OneClick replaces guesswork with verified profiles, transparent pricing, secure payments, and ratings you can actually trust.

Our Mission

To make trusted home services accessible to every household in Nepal — one click away — while creating dignified, steady income opportunities for local professionals.

Our Vision

A future where every city and village in Nepal has instant access to reliable home services, and where "OneClick" becomes the everyday word for getting things fixed, cleaned, and cared for.

Our Journey So Far

2022
Idea

A small team of friends sketched the first concept of a one-stop home service platform for Nepal.

2022
Research

Hundreds of conversations with households and local professionals shaped the platform's core features.

2023
Development

The first version of OneClick was built, tested, and refined with early provider partners in Kathmandu.

2024
Launch

OneClick officially launched to the public, onboarding verified providers across the Kathmandu Valley.

2025+
Future Expansion

Scaling to new cities across Nepal, with mobile apps and smarter matching on the roadmap.
