01Acceptance of Terms

By accessing or using the OneClick platform, users agree to be bound by these Terms & Conditions. For the purposes of this academic project, continued use of the platform after registration is treated as acceptance of the terms described on this page.

If a user does not agree with any part of these terms, they should discontinue use of the platform.

02Definitions

  • Customer — a user who books a service through OneClick.
  • Provider — a user who offers and delivers a service through OneClick.
  • Booking — a request made by a customer for a provider to perform a service at a scheduled time.
  • Platform — the OneClick website and associated pages built for this project.
  • Service — a home-related task (such as cleaning or repair) offered by a provider through the platform.

03User Accounts

Users are expected to provide accurate and truthful information when creating an account on OneClick, including name, email, and phone number. Users are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of their own password and for all activity that occurs under their account.

04Customer Responsibilities

  • Book services honestly and provide accurate booking details.
  • Treat providers with respect and professionalism.
  • Pay the appropriate amount for services rendered, as agreed at the time of booking.

05Provider Responsibilities

  • Provide quality service in line with what was described on their profile.
  • Maintain professionalism when interacting with customers.
  • Respect confirmed booking schedules and arrive as agreed, where possible.

06Booking Process

For the purposes of this project, a booking is created when a customer selects a service, chooses a provider, and confirms a date and time. The provider may then accept or decline the booking. Once accepted, the booking is considered confirmed and appears in both the customer's and provider's dashboards.

07Payments

OneClick, at a project level, may support the following payment approaches:

  • Cash on Service — payment made directly to the provider upon completion.
  • Digital Payment — where implemented, payment made through the platform before or after service.
  • Future Gateway Integration — in a production implementation, OneClick may integrate with a licensed payment gateway; no such integration is claimed unless actually built into this project.

08Cancellation Policy

Customers or providers may cancel a booking before the provider arrives at the service location. For the purposes of this project, cancellations made close to the scheduled time may be discouraged in the interface, but no penalty system is claimed unless implemented.

09Refund Policy

At a project level, refunds may be considered in cases where a service was not delivered as booked or was cancelled before completion. Actual refund processing would depend on the payment method used and is described here only conceptually for this academic project.

10Ratings & Reviews

Only bookings that have been marked as completed may be reviewed by a customer. OneClick, for the purposes of this project, does not permit fake or manipulated reviews, and reviews should reflect genuine experiences with a provider's service.

11Prohibited Activities

Users of the OneClick platform must not engage in the following:

  • Spamming other users or the platform
  • Creating fake or fraudulent bookings
  • Any form of fraud or deceptive activity
  • Harassment of customers, providers, or staff
  • Impersonating another individual or business
  • Any activity that is illegal under applicable law

The project team is not liable for any loss, damage, or dispute arising from the use of this platform outside of its intended academic context.

14Privacy

Information about how user data may be collected and used within this project is described separately in the Privacy Policy. Users are encouraged to review it alongside these Terms & Conditions.

15Suspension & Termination

Accounts that violate these terms — including engaging in prohibited activities — may be suspended or restricted within the scope of this project. Where applicable, users may be notified of the reason for suspension.
