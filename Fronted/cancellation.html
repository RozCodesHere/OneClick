Cancellation Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This cancellation policy explains how customers and service providers should handle cancellations of bookings made through the OneClick platform.

1. Customer Cancellations

Customers should cancel a booking as early as possible. If a customer no longer needs a requested service, they should cancel the booking before the scheduled service time.

2. Provider Cancellations

Providers should avoid cancelling confirmed bookings unless it is necessary. When a provider cannot complete a booking, the customer should be informed as soon as possible.

3. Before the Scheduled Time

Cancellations made before the scheduled service time should be communicated through the available booking process so that both parties have clear information about the booking status.

4. Repeated Cancellations

Repeated cancellations may affect a user's experience on the platform. OneClick may review unusual or repeated cancellation activity as part of normal platform administration.

5. Academic Project Notice

OneClick is an academic BCA project. This policy describes the intended behaviour of the demonstration platform and does not represent a commercial refund or payment policy.

OneClick currently does not process online payments or payment refunds through the platform.
