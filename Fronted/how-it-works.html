The Journey

Six steps, start to finish

Each step is designed to remove friction — from finding the right person for the job to paying safely once it's done.

STEP 01

Search Service

Tell us what you need — plumbing, cleaning, electrical, and more — along with your location and preferred date.

STEP 02

Choose Provider

Compare ratings, reviews, pricing, and availability side by side. Every profile is verified.

STEP 03

Book Appointment

Pick a time that works for you from the provider's live calendar and receive instant confirmation.

STEP 04

Secure Payment

Your payment is held safely until the job is completed. Pay securely using your preferred payment method.

STEP 05

Provider Arrives

Track your provider's arrival and receive a notification when they are on the way.

STEP 06

Leave Review

Rate your experience and leave feedback to help other customers find trusted providers.
