01Introduction

OneClick is a home service platform developed as part of a semester-level BCA project. It is designed to connect customers with local service providers for tasks such as cleaning, repairs, and other home-related services.

This Privacy Policy explains, for the purposes of this project, how OneClick may collect, use, store, and manage information submitted by users while interacting with the platform. It is written to demonstrate good privacy-conscious design practices and does not represent the privacy policy of a commercially operating company.

This document is an academic project deliverable. Actual production implementation may differ from what is described here.

02Information We Collect

For the purposes of this project, OneClick may collect the following categories of information when a user registers for or uses the platform:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Address
  • Booking information
  • Profile information
  • Provider information (for service providers)
  • Device and browser information

03How We Use Information

Where applicable, information collected through OneClick may be used to:

  • Create and manage user accounts
  • Process and manage bookings
  • Communicate with users regarding their bookings or account
  • Provide customer support
  • Improve the functionality and usability of the platform
  • Support basic security and misuse-prevention measures

04Account Information

When a user registers on OneClick, basic account information such as name, email, and phone number may be stored to allow login, booking history, and communication features to function. Users are responsible for keeping their account credentials confidential.

05Booking Information

Information related to service bookings — such as service type, scheduled date and time, address, and any notes provided by the user — may be stored so that bookings can be created, tracked, and managed between customers and providers.

06Payment Information

Where payment functionality is implemented, payment information is intended to be handled through the applicable third-party payment service provider at the time of integration. OneClick, for the purposes of this project, does not claim to store raw card or payment credentials.

This project does not claim actual integration with any specific payment gateway (such as Khalti or eSewa) unless that integration has been implemented in the codebase.

07Location Information

Location information, such as a service address, may be used when required for service-related functionality — for example, to help a provider identify where a booked service should be performed. Location data is used only where relevant to the platform's core functionality.

08Provider Information

Users who register as service providers may be asked to submit additional information such as service categories offered, experience details, and availability. This information may be shown to customers to help them choose an appropriate provider.

09Cookies

OneClick may use basic cookies or local browser storage to support core functionality, such as keeping a user logged in or remembering simple interface preferences. This project does not use cookies for third-party advertising or cross-site tracking purposes.

10Data Security

OneClick aims to follow reasonable, project-appropriate practices to help protect user information from unauthorized access. However, as this is an academic project, no specific encryption standard, security certification, or compliance framework is claimed unless it has actually been implemented.

11Data Sharing

Customer and provider information may be shared with one another only when necessary for platform functionality — for example, sharing a customer's address with the provider assigned to a booking. OneClick, for the purposes of this project, does not sell user information to third parties.

12Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as an account remains active or as needed to support platform functionality such as booking history. Where applicable, data associated with a deleted account may be removed or anonymized at a project level.

13User Rights

Users may request the following regarding their information, where applicable within the scope of this project:

  • Access to the information associated with their account
  • Update or correction of inaccurate information
  • Deletion of their account and associated information

Account Deletion: At a project level, a user requesting account deletion may do so through account settings or by contacting the OneClick team, after which associated data may be removed from the system.

14Children's Privacy

OneClick, as a project platform, is not intended for use by children. It is designed with the assumption that users are of an appropriate age to independently book or provide home services.

15Third-Party Services

Where OneClick integrates with third-party services (such as maps, payment providers, or notification services), those services may have their own privacy practices. Where applicable, such integrations will only be described accurately based on what has actually been implemented in this project.

16Policy Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time as the OneClick project evolves. Any changes will be reflected on this page along with an updated "Last Updated" date.
